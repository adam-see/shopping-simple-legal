# Delete your Shopping. Simple. account

This page explains how to delete your account for **Shopping. Simple.**, an app published by **See Apps**.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open Shopping. Simple. and sign in.
2. Tap the settings icon at the top of your lists.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and the app returns to the sign-in screen.

## If you cannot open the app

Email **seeapps99@gmail.com** from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your account (email address, sign-in details and display name).
- All lists you own and their items. Lists you own are also deleted for anyone you shared them with.
- Your membership in lists owned by other people. Those lists stay for their owners.
- Invites you created and your profile.
- Your Remove ads purchase record on our servers, and the customer record at our purchase provider (RevenueCat).

## What is kept

- Purchases you made in Google Play or the App Store stay with those stores. They remain yours, and you can restore a Remove ads purchase on a new account with the same store account.
- Backups can hold deleted data for a limited time before they are overwritten.

See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy) for details on how we handle your data.
