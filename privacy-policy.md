# Privacy Policy for Shopping. Simple.

**Effective date:** November 24, 2026

Shopping. Simple. ("the app") is a shopping list app for Android and iOS published by **See Apps** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who receives it, and the choices you have.

Contact: **seeapps99@gmail.com**

## Summary

- You need an account (email and password, or Google sign-in) so your lists can sync across your devices and be shared with people you invite.
- Your lists are stored on our backend so you can use them on more than one device.
- The app shows ads unless you buy the one-time "Remove ads" purchase. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and may use your device's advertising ID, subject to your consent choices.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## Information we collect

**Account information.** Your email address, and a password if you sign up with email. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email address and a Google account identifier. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are never visible to us in readable form. You can also choose a display name.

**Your content.** The shopping lists, items, quantities and settings you create, and the membership of lists you share. When you share a list, the other members of that list can see your display name and email address, and you can see theirs.

**Purchase information.** If you buy "Remove ads", we record your account identifier, the store it was bought in (Google Play or App Store), the product, and when it was bought or refunded. We do not receive or store your payment card details; payments are handled by Google or Apple.

**Advertising and device information.** When ads are shown, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and information about how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads are only personalized if you give consent in the consent form shown by the app. On iOS, ads are only personalized or tracked across other companies' apps and websites if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

**Technical information.** Our service providers may process standard technical data such as IP address and device type to operate the service, keep it secure and prevent abuse.

We do not knowingly collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

## How we use information

- To create and secure your account and sign you in.
- To store your lists, sync them between your devices and share them with people you invite.
- To provide and restore your "Remove ads" purchase, including on other devices signed in to the same account.
- To show ads in the app to users who have not bought "Remove ads".
- To diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

## Who we share information with

We use the following service providers, who process information on our behalf or under their own terms:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Account authentication and storage of your lists and purchase status |
| Google AdMob / Google User Messaging Platform | Showing ads and collecting consent choices; [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| Google Sign-In | Optional sign-in with a Google account |
| RevenueCat | Processing and verifying in-app purchases; [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) |
| Google Play / Apple App Store | Payments and purchase receipts |

Other members of a list you share can see the list contents and your display name and email address, as described above. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public. We do not sell personal information.

## Your choices

- **Ads and consent:** If your region requires it, you can reopen the ad consent form from Settings ("Ad privacy choices"). On iOS you can change tracking permission in the system Settings. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
- **Remove ads:** A one-time purchase turns off ads for you and for members of lists you own.
- **Sharing:** You can leave a shared list, or remove members from lists you own.
- **Access, correction and deletion:** You can edit your display name and your lists in the app. To access, correct or delete your account and its data, email us at **seeapps99@gmail.com** from the email address on your account. We will act on verified requests within 30 days. Deleting your account deletes your lists and profile; some records may be kept for a limited time where the law requires it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know, delete and correct personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing; we do not sell personal information. To exercise any right, contact us at the address above.

## Retention

We keep your account and lists until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to honor your purchase and for accounting and legal purposes.

## Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and access to stored data is restricted by account. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Ad requests are configured as not child-directed.

## International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed, and material changes will be noted in the app or on this page.
